HOW TO PLAY

Desktop controls

← → or A / D — move
↑ / W / Space — jump
X — shoot (once you have the gun)
Hold jump in the air — hover with the jetpack (once collected)

Mobile controls

On-screen ◀ ▶ pad to move, ⤒ button to jump, 🔫 button to shoot.

Pickups

$ $USDC coins — your score, shown top-right
◆ Gun — lets you shoot; gets stronger automatically as you clear levels (single → twin → spread → missile)
♥ Extra life (up to 9)
☊ Magnet — pulls nearby coins toward you
🎒 Jetpack — hold jump in the air to hover and shoot from above
Springs launch you high; spikes and boss attacks cost a life

Boss fights

Every level ends in a boss guarding the flag. Shoot or stomp it down — the flag won't activate until it's defeated. Bosses get tougher and gain new attacks each level.
